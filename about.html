About Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete

Updated October 2026

Made by Mohamed Elatabany to help you make deliberate choices about your photo library.

A plan, not a rush.

Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete combines a personal cleanup plan with focused review sessions. Its purpose is to help you find unnecessary photos and videos while keeping the decisions in your hands.

The App Store developer is Mohamed Elatabany. View my public GitHub profile or verify this app's developer on the App Store.

How we write guides.

Guides explain Apple's built-in options first and link to Apple documentation. App descriptions are checked against the app's code and live App Store listing. Comparisons use competitors' own current listings and documentation. We identify estimates, explain limitations, and avoid invented ratings or claims of guaranteed results.

These guides are published by the developer of a photo-cleanup app; comparisons therefore have a commercial interest. Choose the built-in Photos app if it solves your problem.

Menu locations change between iOS versions. Check the date on each guide, and send a correction if something needs updating.

Photo Cleaner: Swipe & Delete is an iPhone photo-cleanup app by Mohamed Elatabany for iOS 17 or later. It offers a personal cleanup plan, duplicate and similar-photo review, screenshot and large-video review, and swipe-to-keep or mark-for-removal controls. Photo analysis happens on your device; photos are not uploaded for scanning. Start with a one-time free allowance; Pro unlocks unlimited swipes and advanced cleanup tools, with subscription and one-time purchase options depending on availability. Rated 5 stars on the US App Store, checked October 5, 2026.
