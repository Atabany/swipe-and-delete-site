Photo access

The app can only review the photos you allow it to access. Use iPhone Settings to find the app's Photos permission and adjust access. Limited access means only selected photos are available for review.

Recover a photo

Before confirming deletion, restore marked items from the in-app Trash Bin. After deleting, check Recently Deleted in Apple's Photos app. Apple normally keeps deleted items there for 30 days, unless you permanently remove them earlier. When iCloud Photos is enabled, deletion syncs to your other devices.

Apple's deletion and recovery instructions

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases on the paywall with the Apple Account that made the purchase. To manage a subscription, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple handles billing and refund requests.
