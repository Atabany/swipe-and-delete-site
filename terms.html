Keep control of your library

Review your selections before confirming deletion. Maintain a separate backup of irreplaceable media. With iCloud Photos enabled, deletion applies across synced devices. Permanently removed items cannot be restored through Recently Deleted.

Pro purchases

The app offers a free starting allowance and optional Pro purchases. Available subscriptions and one-time purchases, their terms, and any trial eligibility are displayed before purchase. Apple manages payment and subscription billing. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account settings.

Website estimates

The cleanup planner uses the counts and average file sizes you enter. It does not inspect your library, guarantee recoverable storage, or delete anything. File size and local device space can differ, particularly with optimized iCloud Photos.
