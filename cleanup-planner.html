Photo Cleanup Planner — Estimate Storage to Review

Updated October 2026

Estimate the space in unwanted photos, screenshots, and videos, then see which group is worth reviewing first. Everything runs in your browser; no library access or uploads.

2.4 GB in this example

Videos account for 2 GB, photos for 0.3 GB, and screenshots for 0.1 GB.

The math, without the guesswork.

Estimated GB = (photo count × average photo MB + screenshot count × average screenshot MB + video count × average video MB) ÷ 1,000. Categories must not overlap. The default sizes are illustrative inputs, not measured averages.

Use sizes from your own library.

Open a photo or video in Photos and open its information view to inspect its details. Sample a few unwanted items rather than assuming every file is the same size. Videos vary dramatically with duration, resolution, frame rate and recording format.

Check Settings → General → iPhone Storage before and after cleanup. With Optimize iPhone Storage enabled, full originals may already live in iCloud, so deleting total file bytes may free much less local space. Recently Deleted can also retain removed items. Keep its recovery window when you can; permanently deleting an item removes that safety net.

Apple: check device storage · Apple: manage photo storage

Turn the estimate into a safe plan.

  1. Start with the group contributing the most file bytes.
  2. Review items individually and keep anything irreplaceable.
  3. Check iCloud syncing and your separate backup before confirming deletion.
  4. Check actual iPhone storage after Photos has processed the change.
