Privacy information

Updated October 2026

Photo analysis happens on your device. Your photos are not uploaded for scanning or cleanup decisions.

Your Photos library

The app uses Apple's Photos permission to access your authorized library. Duplicate detection, similar-photo review, and cleanup decisions are processed on your device. Photos stored in iCloud may be downloaded by Apple's Photos services when you request them; that is separate from uploading photos to our servers.

Review progress and preferences are stored locally. The app also stores its free-allowance usage locally, including in Keychain. Revoking Photos access prevents further access to your library through the app.

Purchase services

Apple processes in-app purchases. The app uses RevenueCat to manage purchase status and Pro entitlements. Purchase records, app-user identifiers, and relevant device or usage information may be processed by those services to manage entitlements and operate their services. On-device photo analysis does not mean that no information ever leaves the device.

RevenueCat privacy policy · Apple privacy policy

This website and the planner

This website does not include advertising, third-party fonts, or analytics scripts. The cleanup planner calculates estimates in your browser and does not send your entries or photos to us. GitHub Pages hosts the website and can process standard request information needed to serve it.

Download links contain Apple's campaign parameters so aggregate downloads can be attributed in App Store Connect when Apple reports them. The links do not contain your photo data.

Current policy and contact

This page explains the implementation checked on October 4, 2026. The privacy policy currently linked from the App Store remains available. If you email support, your message and contact details are used to respond. Contact atabany.apps@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests to access or delete information held by the developer. Local review data can be reset in the app; purchase-allowance records may persist in Keychain. Apple and RevenueCat maintain their own records under their policies.
